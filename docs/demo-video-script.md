# 1Fi Marketplace demo video (2–5 minutes)

Use this as the recording checklist before submitting the assignment.

1. **0:00–0:20 — Introduction**
   - Open the deployed Shop page.
   - Point out the three Shop options: Top Brands, Nearby Stores, and 1Fi Marketplace.

2. **0:20–0:55 — Marketplace catalogue**
   - Select 1Fi Marketplace.
   - Show the promotional carousel, category filters, search, and the dynamic product cards.

3. **0:55–1:45 — Product and variants**
   - Open `/products/iphone-17-pro`.
   - Change Cosmic Orange, Silver, and Deep Blue; explain that the product image, price, and plans are returned by the API/database.
   - Open one more product such as Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra to show distinct URLs and variants.

4. **1:45–2:35 — EMI plans and checkout**
   - Choose a 12-month plan, then a 36-month plan.
   - Highlight payment, tenure, interest, cashback, and the selected-plan summary.
   - Click Proceed and show the generated checkout reference.

5. **2:35–3:15 — Backend and database**
   - Open `/api/products/iphone-17-pro` and show the JSON response.
   - Open MongoDB Atlas and show the `onefi_marketplace` database with the `products` and `checkoutintents` collections.
   - End on the GitHub repository and deployed Vercel URL.

Before submission, upload the recording to Google Drive or YouTube and set its sharing to **Anyone with the link can view**. Paste that view link alongside the GitHub and deployed-demo links in the form.
